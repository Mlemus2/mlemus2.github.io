AI & Knowledge Portals 40% Cycle Reduction

Replacing Static Manuals With Dynamic CMS & AI Workflows

The Ambiguity: Show teams spent roughly 3 weeks per event building 30 page static manuals, creating version drift, slow onboarding, and $200,000 in external vendor SOW spend.
Stakeholders & Process: Partnered with Event Operations, Brand Success Managers, IT, and SaaS vendors to standardize intake in Sessionboard across 32 events, launch dynamic CMS knowledge portals, and embed Claude, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini, and MCP workflows with human review checkpoints.
Measured Outcome: Cut event setup cycles by 40%, compressed manual production from 3 weeks to a few days across 30 events, and saved $200,000 in vendor SOW costs.
M&A & CTO Rollout 12+ Acquisitions Unified

Consolidating 12+ Acquisitions & Directing a $400M+ Rollout

The Ambiguity: Newly acquired shows operated on disconnected CRMs, billing tools, and event platforms with inconsistent reporting and unclear ownership.
Stakeholders & Process: Partnered directly under the CTO across Engineering, Product, Sales, Finance, and external SaaS partners (Cvent, Sessionboard, a2z, GRIP, Hotel Maps, Las Vegas Monorail) to consolidate 12+ businesses onto Salesforce, HubSpot, Zuora, Qualtrics, and Smartsheet.
Measured Outcome: Delivered a $400M+ enterprise platform rollout across 30+ flagship events and launched Power BI and Tableau NPS and KPI dashboards for executive investment decisions.
Ecosystem Architecture 7 Platforms Governed

Cross Platform Governance, Procurement & GTM AI Enablement

The Ambiguity: Multiple registration, directory, mobile app, lead capture, matchmaking, floorplan, and session tools lacked unified architectural standards and cross functional roadmap alignment.
Stakeholders & Process: Manage 4 platform and product managers, translate VP strategy into a 7 platform roadmap, evaluate vendors and renewals with Procurement, lead M&A show technology assessments and migration risk planning, partner with IT and Data & Analytics on integration mapping and Databricks modeling, and trained HubSpot's Breeze AI agent with a structured library of Marketing and Sales questions.
Measured Outcome: Established clear ecosystem standards, M&A transition playbooks, and cohesive Sales and Marketing lead scoring, attribution, and onboarding execution.
Revenue & O2C Controls >99% Order Accuracy

Reengineering Order to Cash & Voice of Customer Loops

The Ambiguity: Manual handoffs between Sales, Marketing, Billing, Finance, and Operations caused billing friction, inconsistent online contracts, and limited visibility into customer pain points.
Stakeholders & Process: Aligned Finance, Billing, Sales Ops, and Legal to rebuild Order to Cash workflows in Epicor ERP (AR, GL, P&L, subledger controls), deployed online contracts across TMM, a2z, and Zuora, and owned Alchemer customer feedback programs.
Measured Outcome: Exceeded 99% order accuracy with working familiarity in SOX IT controls and ASC 606, turning customer survey friction into measurable product improvements.
AI Analytics & GTM Enablement Event Health Score

Turning Raw Post Show Survey Datasets Into an AI Event Health Score

The Ambiguity: Marketing and Sales received post show survey feedback as raw, disconnected datasets that were difficult to interpret or act on.
Stakeholders & Process: Trained Claude with specific analytical criteria to correlate post show survey feedback with mobile app usage and translate complex datasets into plain English insights.
Measured Outcome: Delivered a humanized readout that Sales and Marketing teams immediately act on, powering an Event Health Score that tells the full picture.
PMO & Smartsheet Automation Creative Intake & KPIs

Automating Creative Services Intake, Routing & Delivery KPIs

The Ambiguity: Marketing teams requested creative work through ad hoc emails, causing routing bottlenecks, zero requester visibility, and no delivery time tracking.
Stakeholders & Process: Built an automated Smartsheet intake and ticket routing system with smart routing rules, automated task assignment by portfolio, approval routing, and a centralized creative repository.
Measured Outcome: Eliminated manual email routing, gave internal teams live status visibility, and gave Creative Services clear time to deliver KPIs and bottleneck tracking.
